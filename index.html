Your Host

Hi, I'm Moritz

I am the head and heart behind ZOLT ARENA and hosting is what I love most. I've enjoyed having guests for as long as I can remember. What I care about is simple: that you walk in and feel looked after.

My apartments aren't staged. They're warm, lively homes you can truly settle into, where comfort always comes first.

My Philosophy

Three simple ideas guide every apartment — and every stay.

01

Design

Lived-in, not staged. Old-building charm, clean lines and pieces chosen one by one — rooms that feel composed rather than decorated, and calm from the first step inside.

02

Warmth

Every apartment has its own soul. Soft light, natural tones and quiet corners invite you to slow down — warmth you can feel, not just see.

03

Vintage

Flea-market finds, bentwood chairs and objects with a past. The little imperfections are the point — they make a stay personal.
